How to Use Liquid Dishwash Gel:
  1. Dilute: Mix 1 teaspoon (approx. 5ml) of Maxyshine Dishwash Gel in a small bowl of water.

MAXYSHINE Liquid Laundry Detergent Gel

Give your clothes superior stain removal and lasting softness with Maxyshine Liquid Laundry Detergent. Unlike harsh washing powders that fade dark clothes and leave chalky white patches inside washing machines, our 100% water-soluble gel penetrates deep into fabric fibers to lift collar grime, sweat, and food stains while locking in vibrant colors.

Tough on Stains

How to Use Liquid Laundry Detergent:
  1. Machine Wash (Top & Front Load): Pour 1 capful (approx. 40ml) into the detergent dispenser tray for a standard load (1.5 capfuls for heavy loads).
  2. Direct Stain Pre-Treat: Dab a drop of gel directly onto collar or food stains, rub gently, and wash.
  3. Bucket Wash: Mix half a capful in a bucket of water, soak clothes for 15 minutes, and rinse clean.
